      03 · Reading the evidence

      Different documents answer different questions.

      1. Operational procedures & implementing norms

        The strongest starting point for actions, routing and sequence. A national legal role still needs to be matched to a local desk.

      2. Applicable statutes & adopted local rules

        Authority, conditions, duties and deadlines. Legal responsibility and the person doing the work may differ.

      3. Local guidance & forms

        Submission channels, requested information and local arrangements. Form fields alone do not establish internal checks.

      4. Output records & reports

        Evidence that activity took place and its reported scale. A report does not validate every step in the map.

      Recency is about the applicable version.

      The grant map follows an adopted 2025 local regulation. A 2026 national amendment means its applicability to a new call needs checking. A newer draft does not automatically replace an adopted rule.

      More URLs do not mean more independent evidence.

      A regulation and its annexes share an origin. Reprints and translated copies do not count as independent confirmation.

      The gaps are part of the result.

      Local desk assignments, software behaviour and several handoffs remain unknown. The questions attached to each workflow are the agenda for counterpart review.

      These are source-located research claims, not municipality-validated workflows. Passages were read and referenced manually; independent quote verification has not been completed. One recruitment sheet is historical, and national requirements do not establish current local execution.
