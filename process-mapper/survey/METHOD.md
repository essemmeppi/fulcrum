# Worldwide public-document opportunity screen

Question: in which countries can Fulcrum find public documents that provide a promising starting point for a whole-service blueprint, including internal work, before government collaboration?

Universe: all 197 entries in the existing country registry. One country is one national-government opportunity, not one buyer and not evidence that all its services are mappable. Regional and municipal governments are a separate future study; incidental examples are leads only and excluded from national counts. Counts are evidence-supported lower bounds, not revenue TAM or validated mapping accuracy.

## Two measures, no composite score

- Depth: public-facing information; some internal detail; detailed operational procedure/model.
- Breadth: named services, authorities and service areas for which evidence was actually inspected. One document containing several subjects is not automatically several demonstrated services.

Six search areas: social services; revenue and business; identity and rights; land and environment; public services (education, health, utilities); government operations (procurement, grants, inspections, complaints and appeals).

## Research sequence

1. Worldwide discovery: search every country for public operational documents across the service areas. Save exact queries and search results. A country with only this step completed remains unclassified, not a negative finding.
2. Verify candidates: open official repositories and individual documents. Read procedural passages; record actor/action/sequence/decision evidence, government level, service area, document date and access limitations. An index, a search snippet or a publication duty alone is a lead, not strong evidence. Historical documents demonstrate publication but need a currency warning.
3. Follow gaps: use native-language terms and agency sites; inspect public manual indexes, sitemaps, linked attachments, search-indexed PDFs, audit/procurement archives and existing disclosure logs. No new disclosure requests or government contact. Publicly reachable files outside navigation are in scope; access controls are not bypassed. Record unperformed checks honestly.
4. Report: strong evidence, promising evidence, public-facing evidence only, or unknown. A strong entry requires a named national service and a readable official operational document with internal actors, actions and sequence/decisions. Wider breadth requires separate inspected examples. Do not extrapolate municipal evidence to national government.

The six-area screen is complete only when each area has a recorded check. Initial discovery alone is not a six-area assessment. Unknown includes unsearched, insufficient verification and blocked access; report those reasons separately. Search saturation is bounded by the recorded method, never proof that all public documents were found.

## Relationship to earlier work

The detailed `countries/*.json` profiles and their seven-layer tiers are legacy leads. The opportunity count uses verified service evidence, not those tiers. The validation study in `../research/assumption-1-validation.md` held procedural sources out for experiments; the production screen includes them. Model judges and quote matching do not establish agency validation. Document dates and legal versions must be reconciled before executing a workflow.

Working evidence and search logs are saved under `screening/`. No inference of live digital-twin feasibility is made from document availability.
