# Deta: municipal process evidence portfolio

CDIT member town hall · ADR Vest strategy · 31 October 2026

This pass identifies **52 provisional families/leads**, plus **7 enabling-function search targets**. It produces **10 partial workflow sheets across 8 families**, with **87 source-located action claims** and **22 explicit supported links**. None is municipality-validated. There is no defensible denominator for all municipal processes yet.

## Main findings

The best local mapping candidate is now nonprofit grants: an adopted 2025 regulation, evaluation checklist, reporting/payment forms and a 2025 output report. The latter lists 11 contracts totalling 220,000 lei allocated; it does not independently validate each workflow step. Keep this map tied to the 2025 edition until the 2026 national amendment and local call have been reconciled.

Information-access norms and petitions legislation provide internal legal routing and exceptions. Deta-specific actors, software, actual handling times and automation are still unknown. The 2024 petitions amendment adds an anonymous-harassment exception: old copies that close every anonymous petition miss a real branch.

The 2025 transparency report records 130 publicly announced drafts, four debates and 27 public meetings, with zero recommendations reported. These are self-reported figures, not proof of participation quality or compliance. The PDF includes spreadsheet errors/odd layout; validate counts with the owner before using them for investment scoring.

A 2026 catering procurement publishes internal norms, but they are an untranscribed scan. This is a high-priority acquisition lead, not an absence of internal guidance. Several department pages have only a title; other department pages carry unrelated repeated announcements. Navigation and boilerplate must not be extracted as workflow.
