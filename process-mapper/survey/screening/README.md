# National screening — work in progress

Read `../METHOD.md` for the agreed scope. This is a national-government study of all 197 registry countries. Regional and municipal governments require a separate study.

`countries.json` is the complete working ledger. `searches/` preserves exact queries and raw discovery results. Search results are leads: they do not establish readable procedures or map accuracy. Countries remain unknown until source verification supports a classification. A discovery search does not complete the six-area screen.

The older seven-layer country profiles and `../report.md` are preliminary legacy material; they are not the new opportunity count. No worldwide figure is ready for external use yet.

Initial checks also opened national manual indexes in Australia, Canada, New Zealand and the UK. Those indexes require individual procedural-document checks before being counted as strong evidence. No new paid API research runs have been launched.
