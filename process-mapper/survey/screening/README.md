# National screening

Start with [report.md](report.md) for results and limitations, or [countries.md](countries.md) for the complete 197-country table. Rebuild them from reviewed evidence with `python -m survey.screening.build_report` from `process-mapper/`.

Read `../METHOD.md` for the agreed scope. This is a national-government study of all 197 registry countries. Regional and municipal governments require a separate study.

`countries.json` is the complete working ledger. `searches/` preserves exact queries and raw discovery results. Search results are leads: they do not establish readable procedures or map accuracy. Countries remain unknown until source verification supports a classification. A discovery search does not complete the six-area screen.

The older seven-layer country profiles and `../report.md` are preliminary legacy material; they are not the new opportunity count. The new report gives an evidence-supported lower bound; verification and language coverage remain incomplete.

Positive entries link to individually inspected procedural sections. Index-only and blocked links remain unverified leads. The full logs document the discovery and verification work; a search attempt is never treated as proof that documents do not exist. No new paid API research runs have been launched.
